# Portfolio wording

## Resume project entry (honest version)

**5G Network Intelligence — Offline AI Prototype | Python, Pandas, scikit-learn, Google Colab**

- Built a reproducible pipeline over 82 eligible client-measurement sessions, engineering 33 predictors and training two HistGradientBoosting throughput regressors plus a KPI-proxy warning classifier.
- Evaluated 5s/15s throughput forecasts against persistence on a previously inspected 21-session development partition; observed 18.63% and 9.34% lower MAE respectively (not independent test performance).
- Implemented offline CSV inference, non-negative throughput outputs, persistence-based alert episodes and KPI-based diagnostic hints; smoke-tested 622 rows and generated 12 alert episodes.
- Audited cross-dataset transfer on NordicDat and documented that the model underperformed persistence, identifying a substantial domain-generalisation limitation.

## Short LinkedIn/GitHub description

An offline 5G client-measurement intelligence prototype for short-horizon downlink forecasting and KPI-derived degradation warnings. Includes reproducible session-based evaluation, an uploaded-CSV inference workflow, alert episode grouping, and a documented negative cross-domain transfer result.

## Avoid these claims

Do not write “real-time 5G network monitoring”, “operator-grade fault prediction”, “production-ready”, “verified root-cause analysis”, “99% accuracy”, or “predicts live gNB failures”.
